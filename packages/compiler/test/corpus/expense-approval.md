# Northwind Logistics — Employee Expense Approval Policy (v3)

This policy tells the finance desk how to approve or reject employee expense reports. Limits are company rules.

## Background

Finance has run this process since 2021. Questions go to the finance lead.

## Rules

**E1 Receipts.** An expense over $25 is only reimbursable if a receipt is attached. If there is no receipt, the expense is rejected.

**E2 Submission deadline.** Expenses must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase. Anything submitted later is rejected.

**E3 Meals.** Meals are reimbursed up to $60 per person per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

**E4 Travel.** Air travel must be booked in economy. A business-class ticket is rejected unless the trip is longer than 6 hours.

**E5 Gifts.** A gift to a client worth more than $100 requires approval from the Compliance Office. The finance desk does not approve these itself.

**E6 Repeat submitters.** An employee with 3 or more rejected reports in the last 90 days is referred to the Finance Manager before any other check.

**E7 Large expenses.** Any expense above $5,000 needs Director approval; the desk escalates to the Director.

## Order of review

Review each report in this order: repeat submitters, receipts, deadline, then the category rules.

## Conduct

Do not tell employees whether a report will be approved before review is complete.
