# Halvorsen Freight — Claims Desk Manual (Rev 7)

Internal use only. This manual describes house rules of the Halvorsen Freight claims desk. It is not a public tariff and does not reflect any industry standard.

## 1. Order of review

**Order of review.** Review every claim in this order, and stop at the first rule that decides it: (1) hazmat check, (2) fraud screen, (3) filing window, (4) exclusions and conditions for the claim type, (5) payout calculation, (6) manager threshold. An earlier rule always wins over a later one, so a claim that would be denied under a later rule is still escalated if an earlier rule escalates it.

## 2. Hazmat (R1)

**Hazmat claims.** Any claim involving freight whose commodity class is hazmat is escalated to Hazmat Compliance, regardless of the amount, the claim type or the filing date. The claims desk never pays or denies a hazmat claim itself.

## 3. Fraud screen (R2)

**Fraud screen.** If the claimant has filed 3 or more earlier claims in the previous 90 days, escalate the claim to the SIU (Special Investigations Unit) before applying any other rule except the hazmat check. This applies even if the claim is outside its filing window. Do not tell the claimant that a fraud screen was triggered.

## 4. Filing windows (R3)

**Filing window: damage.** A damage claim must be filed within 9 days after delivery. Days are counted in calendar days from the delivery date to the filing date.

**Filing window: damage noted on the POD.** If the damage was noted on the proof of delivery (POD) at the time of delivery, the filing window for that damage claim is extended to 30 days after delivery.

**Filing window: loss.** A loss claim (freight that never arrived) must be filed within 60 days after the scheduled delivery date.

**Filing window: delay.** A delay claim must be filed within 5 days after the actual delivery date.

**Filing window: temperature.** A temperature claim must be filed within 4 days after delivery. A claim filed after its window is denied as late.

## 5. Exclusions and conditions (R4)

**Packaging exclusion (damage).** A damage claim is denied when the shipper packed the freight and the packaging was inadequate. Freight packed by Halvorsen is never excluded for packaging.

**Minimum delay.** A delay claim is only payable when delivery was at least 2 days late. A delay of less than 2 days is denied.

**Weather delays.** When the delay was caused by a weather event, the first 3 days of delay are not compensable. A weather delay of 3 days or less is denied; a longer weather delay is compensated only for the days beyond the first 3.

**Delay compensation.** Delay claims are paid at $85 per compensable day, up to a maximum of 5 days. No deductible and no declared-value logic apply to delay claims.

**Temperature claims.** Temperature claims are only payable for perishable freight; for any other commodity class the claim is denied. The reefer temperature log must be available to verify the excursion; if no log is available, escalate to the Cold Chain Desk. An excursion of 6 hours or less above the set temperature is within tolerance and is denied. An excursion of more than 6 hours is paid as a total loss (100% damage).

## 6. Payout calculation (R5)

**Liability rates per pound.** The liability cap is the affected weight in pounds multiplied by the rate for the commodity class: general $2.50 per lb, electronics $4.00 per lb, fragile glass $1.75 per lb, perishable $2.50 per lb.

**Base value.** With a Full Value Coverage endorsement on the bill of lading, the base value is the declared value. Without it, the base value is the lesser of the declared value and the liability cap; if no value was declared, the base value is the liability cap. For a damage claim, the loss is the base value multiplied by the damage percentage, minus any salvage value; if nothing remains the claim is denied as having no net loss. Loss and temperature claims are treated as 100% damage with no salvage.

**Deductible.** A deductible of $150 per claim is subtracted from the net loss unless the shipment has Full Value Coverage, in which case there is no deductible. If the net loss does not exceed the deductible the claim is denied. A damage claim with less than 100% damage that is paid is a partial settlement; all other paid claims are approved.

## 7. Manager threshold (R6)

**Manager threshold.** If the computed payout is more than $25,000, escalate to the Claims Manager; the desk never approves more than $25,000 on its own authority. This check comes last, so a high-value claim that is outside its filing window is denied, not escalated. Escalated claims are not paid by the desk (payout 0).

## 8. Background

**About the claims desk.** Halvorsen Freight is a regional less-than-truckload carrier. The claims desk handles cargo damage, loss, delay and temperature claims from shippers and consignees, Monday to Friday.

**Bill of lading.** The bill of lading (BOL) is the contract of carriage. It records the shipper, consignee, commodity description, weight, any declared value and any endorsements such as Full Value Coverage.

**Proof of delivery.** The proof of delivery (POD) is signed by the consignee at delivery. Visible damage should be written on the POD at that time; a clean signature means no damage was noted.

**Salvage.** Salvage value is what damaged goods can still be sold for. Salvage buyers are arranged by the claimant and the offer is documented in the claim file.

**Reefer units.** Refrigerated trailers (reefers) record a temperature log. The log is downloaded by the terminal after delivery and attached to the claim file.

**Contact and hours.** Claimants can reach the desk by phone or email. Claim acknowledgements are sent within one business day. Claim numbers begin with HF-CL.

**Palletized freight.** Palletized freight should be stretch-wrapped and banded. Terminal staff photograph pallets at pickup and delivery when damage is visible.

**Weather events.** A weather event is a severe storm, flood, blizzard or road closure documented by the terminal manager. Ordinary rain or traffic is not a weather event.
