# Employee Travel and Expense Policy
## 1. Reimbursement
1.1 Employees may claim reimbursement for eligible travel expenses if receipts are submitted within 30 days.
1.2 Expenses exceeding ₹50,000 require manager approval.
1.3 Alcohol purchases are never reimbursable.
1.4 Hotel rates should be reasonable for the city of travel.
1.5 Employees travelling internationally must obtain approval before booking.
1.6 Meal expenses above ₹2,000 are denied unless the employee is on an approved client trip.
