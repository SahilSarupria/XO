# Product Warranty Policy
## 1. Coverage
1.1 The warranty period is 24 months from the date of purchase.
1.2 A claim within the warranty period is approved if the defect is a manufacturing defect and proof of purchase is provided.
1.3 Damage caused by misuse is not covered.
1.4 If the product has been repaired by an unauthorised party, the warranty is void unless the repair was approved in writing by the manufacturer.
1.5 Replacement is offered only when repair is not possible within 30 days.
1.6 Claims over 5,000 USD must be referred to the warranty committee.
