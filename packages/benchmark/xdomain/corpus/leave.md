# Employee Leave Policy
## 1. Annual Leave
1.1 Full-time employees may take up to 20 days of annual leave per year.
1.2 Leave requests longer than 5 days require line manager approval.
1.3 If an employee has less than 90 days of service, annual leave is not permitted unless the HR director approves.
1.4 Leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance.
1.5 If the leave balance is insufficient or the request overlaps a blackout period, the request must be rejected.
## 2. Sick Leave
2.1 Employees must provide a medical certificate when absent for more than 3 consecutive days.
2.2 Sick leave is never deducted from annual leave.
