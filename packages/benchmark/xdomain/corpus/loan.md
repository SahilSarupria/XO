# Equipment Loan Policy
## 1. Eligibility
1.1 Staff may borrow equipment for up to 30 days.
1.2 If the equipment value exceeds 2000 USD, department head approval is required.
1.3 Loans are not permitted for contractors, except when a sponsor employee signs the loan form.
## 2. Returns
2.1 If equipment is returned more than 7 days late, the borrower is suspended from borrowing for 90 days.
2.2 When equipment is returned damaged and the damage is not caused by normal use, the borrower must pay the repair cost.
