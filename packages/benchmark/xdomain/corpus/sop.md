# Incident Response SOP
## 1. Detection
1.1 The on-call engineer must acknowledge the alert within 15 minutes.
1.2 The on-call engineer must open an incident ticket and assign a severity.
## 2. Escalation
2.1 If severity is 1 or the outage lasts more than 60 minutes, notify the engineering director.
2.2 After resolution, the incident owner must write a post-incident report within 5 business days.
## 3. Definitions
3.1 Severity 1 means a complete outage of a customer-facing service.
