# Facility Access Policy
## 1. Entry
1.1 If the badge is missing or the visitor is unregistered, entry must be denied.
1.2 If the badge is valid and the visitor is escorted by a staff member, entry is permitted.
1.3 If the badge is valid, the visitor is registered, the area is open, and the escort is present, entry may proceed.
1.4 If the badge is valid, the visitor is registered, approve the entry.
