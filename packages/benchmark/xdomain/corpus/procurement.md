# Procurement Approval Policy

## 1. Approval Limits

1.1 Purchases up to 1,000 USD may be approved by the department manager.
1.2 Purchases above 1,000 USD and up to 10,000 USD require approval from the finance director.
1.3 Purchases above 10,000 USD require approval from the board.
1.4 A purchase must not be split into smaller orders to avoid an approval threshold.
1.5 Purchases from a vendor that is not on the approved vendor list must be approved by the procurement head regardless of value.
1.6 Purchases up to 1,500 USD may be approved by the department manager.
