# Software Services Agreement

## 1. Payment

1.1 The Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt.
1.2 If payment is late by more than 15 days, the Supplier may suspend the service.
1.3 Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## 2. Termination

2.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days written notice.
2.2 The Supplier shall not terminate for convenience during the first 12 months unless the Customer is in material breach.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 Each party must keep the other party's confidential information secret and must not disclose it to third parties.
