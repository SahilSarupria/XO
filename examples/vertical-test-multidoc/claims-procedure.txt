CLAIMS PROCEDURE

Upon discovery of any loss or damage that may give rise to a claim under
this policy, the insured shall notify the police immediately and obtain a
crime reference number.

The insured shall notify the insurer of the loss within 48 hours of
discovery, providing a written description of the property lost or
damaged and the circumstances of the loss.

The insurer shall acknowledge receipt of a claim within 5 business days
and shall appoint a loss adjuster to investigate the claim where the
estimated value exceeds the threshold stated in the Schedule.

The insured shall provide the insurer with any evidence, receipts, or
documentation reasonably required to substantiate the value of the claim,
and shall permit the insurer's representatives to inspect the premises.

The insurer shall settle or decline a claim within 30 days of receiving
all information reasonably required to assess it, and shall notify the
insured in writing of the reasons for any decline.
