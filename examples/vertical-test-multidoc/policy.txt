BURGLARY AND THEFT POLICY — SUMMARY OF COVER

This policy insures the property described in the Schedule against loss or
damage caused by burglary, attempted burglary, or theft involving forcible
and violent entry to or exit from the premises.

The insurer shall indemnify the insured for the value of property lost or
damaged, up to the sum insured stated in the Schedule, subject to the
conditions and exclusions set out below.

The insured shall take all reasonable precautions to safeguard the insured
property and shall keep all locks, alarms, and security devices in good
working order at all times.

Cover under this policy does not extend to loss or damage occurring while
the premises are left unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days, unless
the insurer has given prior written consent.

The insured shall notify the insurer in writing within 30 days of any
material change to the risk, including a change of occupancy or a change
in the security arrangements at the premises.
