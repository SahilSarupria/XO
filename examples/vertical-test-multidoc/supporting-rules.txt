SUPPORTING RULES AND DEFINITIONS

"Forcible and violent entry" means entry to the premises involving
visible physical damage to a door, window, wall, roof, or other part of
the building structure, evidenced by marks left by tools, weapons, or
physical force.

"Premises" means the building described in the Schedule, together with
its permanently fixed fittings, but excluding any garden, yard, or
outbuilding unless separately specified in the Schedule.

The insurer shall not be liable for loss or damage caused by theft
committed by any person lawfully residing at or employed at the
premises, unless that person's involvement is established by a criminal
conviction.

Where a sum insured is expressed as "first loss," the insured shall not
be required to maintain full-value cover, and the insurer shall not
apply average (proportional) reduction to a claim settled under that
sum.

Any dispute as to the amount of a claim, but not as to liability, shall
be referred to arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause
stated in the Schedule.
