# New Starter Onboarding

A short guide for a manager welcoming a new team member.

## Before day one

1. Order the laptop and the access badge.
2. Create the email account and add it to the team list.
3. Book a welcome lunch for the first week.

## House rules

- **Never share your password.** IT will never ask for it.
- **Lock your screen when you leave your desk.** Visitors walk through the office.
- You should file expenses within 30 days.

## First-week checklist

- [ ] Laptop handed over and working
- [ ] Badge collected
- [x] Welcome lunch booked
